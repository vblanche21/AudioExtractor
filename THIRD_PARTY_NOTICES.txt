THIRD-PARTY SOFTWARE: FFMPEG / FFPROBE
=====================================

Audio Extractor invokes ffmpeg and ffprobe as separate subprocesses. The
Windows binary distribution used by BUILD_WINDOWS.bat is 'FFmpeg release
essentials', distributed by Gyan Doshi (https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/).
At the time this kit was prepared, the distribution provider stated its
Windows static builds are licensed under GNU GPL version 3 (GPLv3).
